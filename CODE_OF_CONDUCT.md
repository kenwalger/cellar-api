# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project is a place to learn. We want everyone who takes part, whether a first-time contributor or an experienced developer, to have a respectful, harassment-free experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are still learning.
- Give constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before drawing conclusions.
- Accept correction gracefully and own your mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer privately, using the contact method listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) once it has been filled in. The maintainer will review reports promptly and confidentially. Depending on what happened, the response may be a warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
